# Curbside: Bus Jam Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

This policy explains how Finnova Studios and the services it uses handle information in the Android game Curbside: Bus Jam.

## Game progress

The game runs without an account. It saves your progress, coins, medals, and settings in Android's private app storage so you can resume play. Finnova Studios does not receive this game data. If Android backup is enabled, your device's backup service may keep a copy and restore it when you reinstall or move to a new device.

## Advertising and consent

The game uses Google AdMob for rewarded and interstitial ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK can collect and share your IP address, device or account identifiers (including the Android advertising ID when available), interactions such as app launches, taps, and video views, and diagnostic information such as crashes or performance data. An IP address can also be used to estimate a general location. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention.

The game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to manage advertising consent where applicable. When the game offers a **Privacy options** control, you can use it to revisit your choices. You can also manage or delete your advertising ID in your Android privacy settings. For information about how Google handles this data and the controls it provides, see [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Retention and deletion

Game progress and settings stay on your device until you clear the game's storage in Android settings or uninstall it. If Android backup is enabled, manage any retained backup through your Android or Google Account backup settings. Finnova Studios does not keep a separate copy of your progress or advertising identifiers. Google handles the retention and deletion of advertising and consent information under its own policies. See [How Google retains data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention) and [Google Account privacy controls](https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy).

## Contact

For questions or requests about this policy, contact Finnova Studios through the [privacy policy support page](https://github.com/HappyDeswal/Curbside-Bus-Jam-Privacy-Policy/issues/new). The form is public. Keep personal information and device identifiers out of your post.
